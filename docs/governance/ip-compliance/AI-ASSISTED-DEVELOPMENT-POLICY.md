# AI-Assisted Development Policy

AI tools are assistants and are not owners of PMFreak code. The human contributor remains responsible for reviewing output for correctness, security, provenance, suspicious similarity, extensive snippets, and compatibility with repository policies.

Do not paste confidential, customer, personal, secret, or third-party restricted information into unapproved tools. Do not assume generated output is original or safe to use. Preserve existing security and license controls.

Tool attribution in commits is an operational decision and is not a repository license. Co-authored-by trailers for tools do not transfer ownership and do not require rewriting existing history. Future commits may omit tool trailers if internal policy permits, but contributors must not falsely claim exclusive human authorship where a contract, customer requirement, jurisdiction, or provider term requires disclosure. Legal or contractual review may be required.
